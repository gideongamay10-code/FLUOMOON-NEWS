Marcos Urges Filipinos to Prepare Buckets, Collect Rainwater Ahead of Super El Niño

By FLUOMOON News Desk · October 2026 · 5 min read

President Ferdinand “Bongbong” Marcos Jr. has encouraged households to prepare containers for rainwater collection as the country prepares for possible water shortages linked to a strong El Niño.

FULL REPORT

Households encouraged to prepare for possible water shortages

President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr. urged Filipinos to prepare buckets and other containers to collect rainwater in anticipation of the possible effects of a strong El Niño. The advice was reported following his media engagements in Singapore on October 11, 2026.

Why is rainwater collection being encouraged?

El Niño is a climate pattern involving unusually warm sea-surface temperatures in the central and eastern tropical Pacific Ocean. It can influence weather patterns and bring below-normal rainfall and prolonged dry conditions to parts of the Philippines.

These conditions may put pressure on water supplies, agriculture and communities that depend on regular rainfall. The actual effects can vary by location and season.

What did the President say?

Marcos encouraged households to collect rainwater when it rains. He also said the government intended to provide public information on collecting, filtering and treating the water safely.

Palace officials later described the advice as part of broader preparations for the potential effects of El Niño. The Department of Social Welfare and Development was also reported to be preparing an instructional demonstration on water filtration.

Other preparations being considered

  • Water security: Encourage water conservation, rainwater collection and appropriate reuse.
  • Agriculture: Prepare farmers and assess possible effects on crops and irrigation.
  • Health services: Prepare hospitals for prolonged hot and dry conditions.
  • Cloud seeding: Prepare possible operations intended to encourage rainfall where conditions allow.
  • Education: Consider alternative learning arrangements if extreme heat affects classroom conditions.

Important water safety reminder

Collected rainwater is not automatically safe to drink. Water from roofs, gutters and open containers may contain dirt, chemicals or germs. Follow guidance from local health authorities and use an approved treatment method before drinking water. Keep storage containers covered and clean.

Remember: Do not assume that ordinary filtering alone makes rainwater safe to drink. Use verified guidance from the Department of Health or your local water provider.

What should the public know?

The bucket suggestion is one household-level measure, not a complete solution to a national water shortage. Reliable water access also depends on water infrastructure, supply management, local preparedness and government action.

Residents should follow official updates from PAGASA and local authorities about weather conditions, water interruptions and conservation measures in their area.

Why El Niño Can Lead to Drier Conditions in the Philippines

Understand the climate pattern and how it can affect rainfall and water supplies.

Changes in Ocean Temperatures

During El Niño, the central and eastern equatorial Pacific Ocean becomes warmer than average, while waters near the Philippine Sea may be cooler than usual.

These changes influence how heat and moisture move between the ocean and atmosphere. Because tropical rainfall is strongly connected to warm ocean water and rising moist air, the shift in ocean temperatures can alter where clouds and rain develop.

As a result, the Philippines may experience fewer rain-producing weather systems or less frequent rainfall during certain periods.

Effects of El Niño in the Philippines

El Niño may cause below-normal rainfall, warmer temperatures, and longer dry periods in some areas. These conditions can affect agriculture and water supplies.

How Can We Prepare?

People can conserve water, follow local weather advisories, and avoid unnecessary water consumption. Staying informed about official announcements can help communities prepare.

How Families Can Prepare for Possible Water Interruptions

Follow local advisories and plan responsible water use at home.

Why Water Preparation Is Important

Water is essential for drinking, cooking, cleaning, and other household activities. Possible water interruptions can affect families, especially during periods of low rainfall.

How Families Can Prepare

  • Store clean water in covered containers.
  • Prepare enough drinking water for family members.
  • Use water responsibly and avoid unnecessary waste.
  • Check announcements from your local water provider.
  • Keep water storage containers clean and covered.

Water Conservation at Home

Families can conserve water by turning off faucets when not in use, fixing leaking pipes, and reusing suitable water for cleaning or watering plants.

Stay Informed

Residents should follow official announcements from their local government units and water service providers to prepare for possible interruptions.
